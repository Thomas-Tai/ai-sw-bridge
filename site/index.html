How you work with it: human-gated, and quick to try

1. Propose → approve → execute (human-gated safety)

Every change follows the same three-step gate: the agent proposes a spec, you review and approve it, and only then does it execute against your live SOLIDWORKS seat. Nothing runs silently — every step is visible before it touches your model.

2. The real kernel walls

This isn't friction-free, and we say so plainly: a whole class of features — lofts, ribs, wraps, combines and the like — can't be driven out-of-process, so the bridge refuses them cleanly rather than emit a broken part; and in dimensioned mode a handful of SOLIDWORKS dimension dialogs still need a human tick before they'll proceed. These walls are labeled honestly, not hidden — see the full, current list in Known Limitations.

3. Try it with no seat

You don't need SOLIDWORKS installed to start. Author and lint a real spec offline — schema, references, locals, DFM lint — with zero license (Tier A) via the 5-minute Quickstart. Building actual geometry is Tier B, and that's where a SOLIDWORKS seat becomes required.
